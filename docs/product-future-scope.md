# Active Product Scope

The only active requirements baseline is the PRL specification supplied in the
pasted text, clauses 1–13. The authoritative requirement-by-requirement status,
gaps, and delivery sequence are maintained in
[client-requirements-gap-plan.md](client-requirements-gap-plan.md).

This document replaces the former broad product wishlist. Items from the old
wishlist that are not needed to satisfy clauses 1–13 are out of scope and
must not be treated as commitments or acceptance criteria. Technical design
suggestions in the gap plan are implementation guidance; they do not add
contractual requirements beyond the pasted specification and clarifications
approved by PRL.

Existing application features may remain in the codebase, but they do not
expand the active scope. Update this scope only when PRL supplies or approves
additional requirements.
